A lin is a lineage, not a list.

Each lin starts with a founder and grows one big/little pair at a time. You might have two bigs, or littles who go on to found families of their own. The tree shows all of it, going back to the very first pairing.

01

Sign in with your Penn Google account. If a board member has already added you, your profile is waiting.

02

Find your big

Search by name, open any lin, and trace your family upward. Propose a link and your big confirms it.

03

Add your littles

Keep the tree growing. Add a personal email before you graduate so your profile stays yours.

About Penn CSA

A social, cultural, and political home for Chinese and Chinese-American life at Penn.

CSA exists to build a network of people interested in Chinese and Chinese-American affairs, and to give everyone a way to learn more about the culture, history, food, and news while becoming part of a multifaceted family of members.
