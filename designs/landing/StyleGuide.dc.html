CSA Lins · Style guide

Stickers on cream.

Cream paper, deep-red ink, vermilion shadows, one gold highlight. Everything is a sticker: thick outline, hard offset shadow, a slight tilt when it is decoration and none when it is interface. These rules cover the landing page, the tree view, the side panel, the login page and admin.

Voice

Warm, direct, a little cheeky. Talk about bigs, littles and lins the way members do. Short sentences. Fun comes from the stickers, not from exclamation points.

Icons & motion

Stroke icons on a 24px grid, 2.5px stroke, round caps, no emoji. Buttons press in (translate to the shadow, shadow disappears). Stickers can wiggle ±2° on hover. Nothing fades or blurs.

Color discipline

Vermilion means "act here". Gold highlights one thing per view. Lin colors belong to nodes, tabs and dots, never to buttons. Everything else is cream, white and deep-red ink.
