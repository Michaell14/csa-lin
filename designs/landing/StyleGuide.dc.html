CSA Lins · Style guide

Paper, ink, and vermilion.

Warm paper backgrounds, near-black ink for text, one vermilion accent for actions and emphasis. Each lin keeps its own color and the interface stays quiet around it. These tokens apply to the landing page, the tree view, the side panel, and the admin pages alike.

Voice

Warm and direct. Talk about bigs, littles, and lins the way members do. Short sentences, no exclamation points, no corporate filler.

Icons

Stroke icons on a 24px grid, 2px stroke, round caps. No emoji in the interface. The three-node tree mark is the only logo element.

Color discipline

Vermilion means "act here" or "this matters." Lin colors belong to trees, tabs, and dots, never to buttons. Everything else is paper and ink.
